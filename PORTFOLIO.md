# LeadFlow AI Portfolio

## AI Automation for Service Businesses

LeadFlow AI focuses on practical automation for businesses that receive leads through social media, websites, forms, messaging apps, and CRM systems.

### Current portfolio project

**AI Lead Qualification Telegram Bot**

A working Make.com automation that demonstrates:

- automated lead qualification
- structured data collection
- conversation state management
- OpenAI fallback handling
- routers and filters
- manager notifications
- human handoff
